Original authored work. A remix of the RPG Toolkit sword mark,
redrawn for TK Reawakened and levelled up into the master logo.

Two marks are acknowledged as references:

1. The sword from the "RPG Toolkit Development System / Version 3" splash
   screen, by Christopher B. Matthews. The winged guard, its triangular
   inlays, the silver blade and the pommel stone follow that design.
2. The animated sword from rpgtoolkit.net, tksword.mp4 and tksword.webm
   (512x512, 121 frames, 30 fps, 4.03 s). The mp4 is byte-identical to the
   Wayback Machine's capture of 2017-01-07 and has not changed since; the
   webm is byte-identical to its capture of 2017-10-09 and has not changed
   since either (an earlier 2017-01-07 capture of the webm was a different
   encode). The digests are INM4YT2LAJUNV4W5NIWUWT3OS423LS7N and
   WNNNN6S65O2X4ULBOHC6KSUG7ZGBL6BE, and the site was still serving both
   unchanged on 2026-10-20. The centre-set guard stone, the ribbon tied at
   the grip and the dark ribbed grip follow that design. The animation's
   author is not recorded on the site.

The animated mark's blade is broken. In this logo it is whole: the blade is
mended.

No pixel data from either reference file is present in this image. It was not
upscaled, filtered or auto-traced from them, and it is not derived from any
legacy binary, game, screenshot or fetched fixture.
