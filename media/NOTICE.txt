Original authored work. A remix of the RPG Toolkit sword mark,
redrawn for TK Reawakened and levelled up into the master logo.

Two marks are acknowledged as references:

1. The sword from the "RPG Toolkit Development System / Version 3" splash
   screen, by Christopher B. Matthews. The winged guard, its triangular
   inlays, the silver blade and the pommel stone follow that design.
2. The animated sword from rpgtoolkit.net (tksword.mp4 / tksword.webm),
   the imagery on the rpgtoolkit.net since 2017-01-07 and byte-identical
   in every capture since (digest INM4YT2LAJUNV4W5NIWUWT3OS423LS7N). The
   centre-set guard stone, the ribbon tied at the grip and the dark ribbed
   grip follow that design. The animation's author is not recorded on the
   site.

The animated mark's blade is broken. In this logo it is whole and glowing: the
blade is mended, and the glow is the point.

No pixel data from either reference file is present in this image. It was not
upscaled, filtered or auto-traced from them, and it is not derived from any
legacy binary, game, screenshot or fetched fixture.

media/icons/ is generated from this file by tools/icons/build.mjs.
