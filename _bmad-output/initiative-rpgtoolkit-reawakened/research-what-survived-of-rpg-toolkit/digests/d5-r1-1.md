## Verdict: did community conflict and forks cause the stall?

**Not supported. Confidence: medium-high.** I found no public evidence of conflict: no hostile issue threads, no notices about disputes, and no competing forks of the same codebase. The one policy debate (#19) reached friendly agreement. RPGWizard was a rename that the same lead carried on with, not a breakaway. The evidence points to other causes:
- **Effort split across parallel rewrites.** Three or four TK4 engines in three languages were started in 2015-16 by a contributor pool of about 5 people, and each stopped within months.
- **Contributors dropping away until one person was left.**
- **The official home went dark** around 2016-17.
- **Repeated compatibility breaks:** TK3 to TK4/JSON (2015), then RPGWizard 1.x (2017), then 2.x/Lua (2021-22). Each break left the previous user base behind.
- **Dependence on an abandoned library** (CraftyJS).

"Forks" is accurate only in this narrow sense of parallel engine experiments inside the same org, not a split community.

## RPGWizard's direction
Yes, it moved away from compatibility. It dropped the TK3 binary formats in 2015-17 (JSON), the TK3 RPGCode language (replaced by a JS API), and in 2.x it plans to drop its own 1.x formats as well (Lua/LÖVE).
