# What survived of aljo productions (round 1, pass 1)

Retrieved 2026-10-10. Sources: Wayback Machine CDX lookups and raw captures (`id_`), plus web search. WA = `https://web.archive.org/web/<ts>/<url>`.

## Short version

Plenty survived. The archives still hold four game profiles on toolkitzone.com, a win in MrG's Shack challenge on mysticsoftware.net, and the aljo brothers' own site at **aljo.afraid.org**. That site includes **John@AlJo's Coder Challenge**, an RPGCode contest site that John ran. Most important, the archive holds **real copies of the Little Man and Bus Driver game zips**. Each one begins with the `PK` zip signature.

## Challenges and contests

- **MrG's Shack "Pea Shooter" contest** (mysticsoftware.net, WA 20040829030339 `.../mrgoldsoft/shack/peahooter.htm`): **1st place went to Little Man (With A Pea Shooter)**. Grey_Havoc's Gothic Mario was 2nd. MrG wrote "congratulations to the Aljo brothers for their first place award".
- **"Slanted and Enchanted"** (`.../shack/slanted.htm`, WA 20040829030304) was the contest Illegal Aliens won.
- **John@AlJo's Coder Challenge** (`aljo.afraid.org/coderchallenge/`, WA 20050207100230 and 2006 captures). The site's rules allowed RPGCode only: no plugins and no external graphics. Its contests were "Lunchroom Linguistics" (Apr 23 2004, stacks and queues, won by KSNiloc) and a "Picture Perfect Painters" drawing contest (`ppp.txt`). Captured zips: `contest2.zip` (177 KB) and `recursiverampage.zip` (4 KB). The footer reads "Site design © John@aljo & Dan Cryer 2003-2004".
- The toolkitzone contest ranking, Expo and edition pages (2007–2010 captures) have **no mention of aljo**.

## Other traces

- **rpgtoolkit.net/member/johnaljo/** (WA 20120520075900): member since 2004. The achievements page (20120520134231) lists the Golden Joystick (first game released, Dec 12 2004), a Stone Sword for 100 posts (Jun 8 2004), a Stone Sword for 5 good reviews (Mar 28 2007), and other awards.
- **TK Rants comics**: 10 files, `tk_rant_aljo_01–11.gif` (dated Mar/Apr 2004), are listed at `tkrants.toolkitzone.com/` (WA 20040516044619). Only 03, 04 and 06 were captured (20040610). The captured 03 is a valid GIF of only 8 KB, while the listing gives 344k, so the capture may not be the original file.
- **Mystic Software review** "The Blobs", reviewer Aljo (WA 20020820031923 `mysticsoftware.net/reviews/theblobs_aljo.htm`). Also `images/banners/aljobanner.gif` (2003).
- aljo.afraid.org was titled "Aljo Productions" (captures from 2004). It also had Meridian 59 and MIDI pages, which are not Toolkit-related.
- toolkitzone forum profile ids: Allan@Aljo u=585, John@aljo u=76.
