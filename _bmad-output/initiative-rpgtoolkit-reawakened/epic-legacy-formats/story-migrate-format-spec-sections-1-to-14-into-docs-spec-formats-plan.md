## Intent

**Problem:** `docs/format-spec.md` §1–§14 (lines 24 to 1515, 1,492 lines) is the byte-level text our format readers were built from. It is now frozen and superseded: its own banner (line 1) says `docs/spec/` is normative and that the body stays only because some of its content exists nowhere else. `docs/spec/formats/` holds twelve pages plus `dos-palette.json` (987 lines today) covering the same layouts, but it lacks what the old file alone records — the code-page round trip, the bounds-check convention, the ASCII fold and its rationale, residue, the size limits, the five §4.8 conversion rulings, the PNG and JPEG decoding rules and the image-kind census, the loose-folder package form, the L1–L26 limits, the distribution manifest and the TK2 Pro refusal, and the §14 checklist — and it contradicts §1–§14 in eighteen places (R-01 to R-16, R-27, plus ADD-1). Two entries are gated on this one: **2.24** changes the readers and the dump to whatever is ruled here (`after = [23]`), and **2.26** puts `docs/spec/examples/` in the dump gate (`after = [24, 25]`). Entry 3.28 cannot delete the old file while its §1–§14 content lives in one place only.

**Approach:** In one doc-only commit (G7), move every §1–§14 fact that `docs/spec/` lacks onto the page that owns the layout, as a block opening with **Reawakened rule** (ADR 0007 decision 2), with the landed-code citation of each rule and every §1–§14 OWNER comment kept. Where the two disagree, `docs/spec` wins by the owner's default of 2026-10-15 (`_bmad-output/initiative-rpgtoolkit-reawakened/discovery/docs-spec-reconciliation.md`, "Owner decisions"), with the four carve-outs: a guard-rail forces the rule (G1/G2/G3), we deliberately added it, an earlier owner ruling recorded it, or the archive corpus contradicts `docs/spec` (ADR 0007 decision 3) — a page corrected by corpus evidence names the files and counts them. R-01 is already ruled. Every other conflict is put to the owner as a proposed ruling with its alternative, in the matrix below, and each names the entry that changes the code afterwards. §9 (entry 3.27) and §15 (entry 16.13) are out of scope.
