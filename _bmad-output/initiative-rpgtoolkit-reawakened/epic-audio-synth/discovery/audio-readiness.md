## Items to confirm at 6.1 to 6.3

1. Whether the Epic 4 effect-record names (`music.play`, `sfx.play`) stay as the effect vocabulary (the applier maps them to tracks) or are renamed in a migration. Recommended and recorded as D-17 in the proposal: keep them, since they are effects, not cues; what changes is who decides same-file and silence (F-02).
2. The legacy meaning of `fademidiin`, `fademidiout` and `setmidivolume` is **[UNCHECKED]**; ask in the next extraction round or record them as not reproduced.
3. Q14 (frame-sound breakdown) is unanswered; frame sounds keep the effect-record behaviour Epic 4 built until answered.
4. Whether the MP3 and OGG transcode-on-import tool is scheduled anywhere: no entry in any epic builds it **[UNCHECKED]**.
5. How a script-set volume (`setvolume`, `mediaVolume`) relates to the player's mix: owner decision D-1 says volumes are settings, but a value a script can read back must be replay-safe core state. Proposed in the proposal as D-19: script volume is core state outside the game save (legacy Q12 and Q13: not saved, not reset), and the player's mix is a runner-side multiplier that never enters core. Entry 6.1 rules it.
