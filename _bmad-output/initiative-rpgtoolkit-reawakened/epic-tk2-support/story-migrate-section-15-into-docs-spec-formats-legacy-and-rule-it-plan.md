## Intent

**Problem:** `docs/format-spec.md` §15 (lines 1516 to 1885, 370 lines) is our only complete description of the Toolkit 2 (2.x) data files, and it was written from the archive's own **real files**, not from reading the original programs (`docs/format-spec.md:1520-1538`). Under ADR 0007 `docs/spec/` is now normative, and the `docs/spec/formats/legacy/` pages describe the same formats from the original programs' **reading logic alone** — `docs/spec/README.md:41` says so plainly: the legacy layouts were "documented from the original programs' reading logic only; no Toolkit 1-2 sample files were available". The two disagree in eight places (`R-17` to `R-24`, `docs-spec-reconciliation.md` section B) and six more questions of §15 have no page at all. Two of the disagreements are already settled in our favour by 15 and 17 real files (the binary `.gam` minor-2 field widths and the text `.anm` frame count), one is settled by the owner's round-5 extraction (round 5 `Q1`, `Q2`), and §15's corpus census, worked examples and seven judgement notes exist nowhere else. Entries 16.4 to 16.7 (the TK2 readers), 16.10 (the refactor sweep) and 3.28 (the citation sweep that deletes the old file) wait on this one, so the formats are unusable until it lands.

**Approach:** In one doc-only commit (G7), merge §15 onto the pages under `docs/spec/formats/legacy/`, and correct the three current pages the §15 corrections touch (`docs/spec/formats/main-file.md`, `docs/spec/formats/tiles.md`, and the legacy pages themselves). Every conflict is ruled in the I/O matrix below as a **legacy fact** (with the real-file count that proves it), a **Reawakened rule** block (a deliberate departure or scope decision, opening with the words **Reawakened rule** per ADR 0007 decision 2), or a **named open question**. Where `docs/spec` is right and §15 is wrong, `docs/spec` stays and §15 changes; where the corpus or round 5 contradicts `docs/spec`, the page is corrected in place and cites the files with a count (ADR 0007 decision 3). The seven `<!-- OWNER: … -->` notes of §15 and the ruling each records are carried over, and the corpus census, the worked examples, the zero-byte-placeholder decision and §15.15's open questions all survive on their new pages.
